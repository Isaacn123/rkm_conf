A MESSAGE FROM

THE HOST

The Location - MIRACLE CENTRE CATHEDRAL GROUNDS

Miracle Centre Cathedral is a Pentecostal megachurch in Kampala, the capital of Uganda. It is one of the largest Christian congregations in East Africa, known for its vibrant services, large weekly attendance, and extensive evangelistic outreach.

meet The Hosts

Robert and Jessica Kayanja are the Lead Pastors of Miracle Centre Cathedral and founders of a global ministry, Robert Kayanja Ministries. Pastors Robert and Jessica are devoted to bringing those far from God back to Him.

Pastor Robert Kayanja is an esteemed pastor, accomplished author, and the visionary founder and president of Robert Kayanja Ministries. He faithfully embodies the message God placed on his heart: to minister love and restoration to a broken world. For nearly four decades, Pastor Kayanja has passionately proclaimed the Gospel of Jesus Christ in at least 80 nations through mass Miracle Crusades. By allowing the Holy Spirit to lead, his ministry is consistently marked by miracles, signs, and wonders.

Today, Pastor Robert is the overseer of the Bucket Project, Miracle Bible College, and Miracle Children’s Outreach, which includes the Never Again Children’s Home and Kapeeka Orphanage and Primary School. He is the founder of Channel 44 Television and several other business ventures, including impactful agricultural projects that empower local communities through sustainable farming initiatives. He also works actively with Afri-Aid and the Karamoja Project, providing essential support, food relief, and long-term development solutions to vulnerable communities across the region.

Pastor Robert’s commitment to transforming lives has earned him renowned awards and recognition for his outstanding humanitarian work, further demonstrating his unwavering dedication to ministering love in practical and life-changing ways.

Pastor Jessica Kayanja is a respected Ugandan pastor, author, philanthropist, and the visionary founder and president of Girl Power Ministries International, a vibrant women’s empowerment movement dedicated to nurturing the holistic woman in spirit, soul, and body. Serving alongside her husband, Pastor Robert Kayanja, at Miracle Centre Cathedral in Kampala, she has devoted her life to ministry, women’s advocacy, and community transformation.

A gifted leader and speaker, Pastor Jessica is celebrated for her bold, fearless, and passionate articulation of the Word of God. She is known for her inspiring books, powerful conferences, and the popular Jessica Kayanja Show and Marriage Now programs, which continue to uplift and transform lives across Africa and beyond.

Holding a Bachelor’s degree in International Relations and advanced theological degrees, Pastor Jessica is also a humanitarian and activist who champions the rights of women and girls, supports disadvantaged communities, and spreads hope through media initiatives like Channel 44 Television.

Together with Pastor Robert, she is blessed with three wonderful children: their son Robert Kayanja Jr., and twin daughters Kirstein and Kristiana, as they continue to minister love, faith, and empowerment to countless lives around the world.

our guest Speakers

PASTOR BENNY HINN

For over 50 years, Pastor Benny Hinn has faithfully proclaimed the Gospel of Jesus Christ with boldness and power, leading multitudes into salvation and igniting countless testimonies of healing through the presence of the Holy Spirit. As he ministers across the globe, lives are transformed through salvation, healing, or the call to ministry.

His counsel is sought by leaders and government officials worldwide, and his teachings continue to equip tens of thousands of ministers while inspiring and mentoring the next generation. Crossing geographic, cultural, and language barriers, Pastor Benny Hinn’s ministry carries a global impact that touches individual hearts in a deeply personal way.

DR. KENNETH COPELAND

In 1966, Kenneth embarked on his ministry journey after God instructed him to attend classes at Oral Roberts University in Tulsa, Okla.

Kenneth Copeland immersed himself in learning about faith and God's Word. Despite financial challenges, he experienced God's provision and peace.

Later in 1967, Kenneth Copeland receives a clear call to ministry while praying in the Arkansas Riverbed. He commits to preaching the gospel to the nations.

Dr. Kenneth and Gloria Copeland Help believers to build their unshakable faith in Christ and live in victory for six decades.

DR. PASTOR PAUL & BECKY ENENCHE

Dr Paul Enenche is the founder and senior pastor of Dunamis International Gospel Centre (DIGC), headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria. Dr Paul, who was trained as a Medical doctor but called into full-time ministry by God, preaches every Sunday to over 100,000 members in Abuja at the Glory Dome and, through the network of satellite churches, to millions in Nigeria and beyond.

Dr Paul Enenche is married to the ever-smiling wife, Dr Becky Enenche, and they have 4 children. Dr Paul has the mandate to restore human destiny and dignity by administering the benefits of redemption through the ministry of the word and the demonstration of power.

OVER 1.500.000 PEOPLE GATHERED FOR THE FIRST TIME IN EAST AFRICA IN A SINGLE VENUE TO WORSHIP THE ALMIGHTY GOD

In June 2025, a healing crusade in Uganda, hosted by Pastor Robert Kayanja at Miracle Centre Cathedral, attracted over 1,500,000 people and featured reported miracles, including healing from cancer and blindness. The three-day event, drawing attendees from 14 nations, emphasized spiritual revival and the Holy Spirit, with 100,000 pastors in attendance.

Pastor Benny Hinn focused on the power of the Holy Spirit, urging believers to seek a deeper relationship with God and to trust in His healing power.
